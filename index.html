Meet Geeta · Founder

Geeta Chauhan, the hand behind every flower

Geeta Creations is Geeta. Every saree carries her eye, her brush and her name. She is the artist who paints it, the designer who imagines it, and the founder who built a label around it from her studio in Jaipur.

“A print can be copied a thousand times. A painted flower happens only once.”

Geeta Chauhan · Founder & artist

The artist

Every stroke is hers

Geeta paints each piece herself, freehand, with a brush. She starts with a bare length of chiffon or kota, sketches a single branch, and lets it grow until the pallu blooms. Petals are built in layers, light to deep, with the softness of a watercolour.

The designer

Every drape is designed

Before the brush touches cloth, Geeta designs the whole saree: the colour story, where the flowers fall on the pallu, how the border frames them, and what shows in the pleats. Her collections draw on Jaipur itself, from the roses of the old baghs to bougainvillea over haveli walls.

The entrepreneur

A label she built herself

Geeta turned a personal art into a brand of her own. She has been painting and sharing her work since at least 2013, and she still runs every part of it: designing collections, painting, custom commissions, meeting clients by appointment and shipping across India.

The Craft

From a bare drape to a painted heirloom

Each piece takes days of slow, careful work. Here is how one of Geeta's sarees comes to life.

The cloth

Fine chiffon, kota doria or organza is chosen for its fall and sheen, then washed and pressed.

The sketch

The motif is drawn freehand, planned around the pallu, border and the pleats that will show.

The frame

The fabric is stretched tight on a wooden frame so every brushstroke lands true.

The painting

Petals and leaves are layered by hand, pale washes first, deep shading and fine veins last.

The finish

Colours are set, edges finished, and the piece is folded in tissue for you.

Caring for painted silk & chiffon

So it blooms for years

Dry clean only

Always dry clean. Never soak, scrub or machine wash a painted piece.

Fold in muslin

Store wrapped in soft muslin and refold every few months along new lines.

Perfume first

Spray perfume before you drape, so it never touches the paint.

Iron on reverse

Press on the back at a low setting, with a thin cloth in between.
